🌱 Before We Start

Do you have a soil-test report from a laboratory?

Why is soil testing important?

Nitrogen (N), phosphorus (P), potassium (K), calcium (Ca), soil pH and other properties cannot be reliably determined just by looking at the soil.
Do you know your soil-test values?

🌱 Farm Information

📋 Soil Test Report

Enter the values exactly as shown on your laboratory soil-test report.

⚠️ Different laboratories may use different extraction methods, units and rating classes. Do not compare values from different reports without checking the laboratory's reference range.

❓ No Soil Test?

That's okay. You can still get general fertilizer guidance based on your crop and soil type.

Important:

Without a soil test, the app cannot reliably tell you whether your field is deficient in phosphorus, potassium, calcium or other nutrients.

🧪 Recommended next step

Take a representative soil sample to a soil-testing laboratory or agricultural extension service. Keep the report and enter its values into this app later.
