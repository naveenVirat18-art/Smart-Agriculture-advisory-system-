Basic Guidance

🐛 Common Problems

🐜 Aphids

Small insects that suck plant sap, often found on young leaves.

🐛 Caterpillar

Leaves may have holes or edges may be eaten.

🪲 Stem Borer

Damage inside stems can cause wilting or dead central shoots.

🦟 Whitefly

Small white insects may fly from the underside of leaves.

🦟 Thrips

Leaves may show silvery patches, distortion or curling.

🍃 Leaf Spot

Brown, black or yellow spots may appear on leaves.

🍂 Blight

Rapid browning or drying of leaves may occur.

🍄 Powdery Mildew

White powder-like growth may appear on leaves.

⚠️ Important: This page provides basic identification and prevention guidance. Do not apply a pesticide only from a photo or this page. For chemical treatment, follow the crop label, local agricultural recommendations and required safety instructions.
